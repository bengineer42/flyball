# Worked examples

Each example is a complete application with its own book. They are the place
to look for real hardware: boards, wiring, drivers and the operating
procedures for a specific rig.

- **Humidity** — a chamber held at a target relative humidity by blending two
  pumps. Raspberry Pi, TB6612 motor driver, SHT4x sensor.
  Book: `examples/humidity/book/`.
