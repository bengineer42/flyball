    PwmPump ¶

    One PwmLink channel, driven 0-1 of full with an optional deadband.

    Real hardware (flyball_linux.links.pwm.PwmConfig, tag pwm) and its fake (fake_pwm) share this protocol, so this driver -- and the blender that uses it -- never touches a real chip in a test.
